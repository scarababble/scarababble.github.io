july 25 (saturday)

8:23 pm


i hope you know i'm taking what you said the other day to heart. i've drawn up a (very) rough 5 year plan, and i'm trying to take care of smaller stuff like cleaning my room and getting my laptop fixed.

i'm resisting the urge to text you some version of "do you still like me?" "do you actually like being around me?" "do you love me?" because if i asked you those things as often as i thought them, i would drive us
both insane.

i've kinda realized in the last couple days that i can't really put off getting a therapist any longer. i've let my mind deteriorate so badly that i can't fix it by myself anymore. i need to get help if i'm going to be a functional person, much less a responsible adult. to put it another way, i think that you (and everyone else
i care about) deserves the version of me that's in therapy and getting better.

i wish i could sit you down and physically talk to you, but i think i'd get all weepy like i always do. maybe i can write you a letter and give it to you. i wonder what's going through your head while you read these... i guess i can't know yet.
