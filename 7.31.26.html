july 31 (friday)

11:17 pm


today was a good day. i got a shirt from the thrift store for pretty cheap, but it was too small. i'm planning on seeing if you want it.

i had a meeting with my academic advisor and it went really well. instead of dropping out, we changed all of my classes to online classes. it's not how i wanted my first semester to go (i don't do great with online school) but i'm really grateful that i get to stay in school while i save up money.

i also finally told you the whole situation with my school stuff, including the fact that i'll be starting online. you seemed really supportive which was a big weight off my shoulders. i actually feel kinda dumb for being so nervous about your reaction. i think that talk we had over the phone was just a "wrong place, wrong time" type of deal. i was incredibly stressed out; you were still in "work mode" (and also incredibly stressed out). i feel better now that we're on the same page.

we've been talking a lot over text the last couple days, especially today. it reminds me of what it was like in highschool, when we would just sit there and text each other for hours. i get kinda nostalgic for those times every once in a while... especially since that was around the time i started realizing i liked you .//////.

anyway, if i had to describe what i'm feeling in one word it would definitely be "relief." this is basically the first time in months where i feel like i'm not being chased by a sabretooth tiger. i almost don't know how to process it- but that is definitely not a complaint.
