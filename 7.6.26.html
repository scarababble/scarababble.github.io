july 6 (monday)

11:09 pm


i submitted my resume again and they rejected it again. the rejection email was the first thing i woke up to, so i was pretty pissed off. i still am, to be honest. resubmitted it for the nth time. i promise my original resumé wasn't anything insane; all of the nitpicks are stuff like "you spaced this incorrectly" or "you bolded this text when you're not supposed to" or "you need to put the sections of your resumé in this very specific order". it literally feels like a bit. like something a corporation would do in a valve game.
one of the jobs i want requires you to apply by the 8th, so if they don't "approve" my resumé by then, they're literally just screwing me over for NO reason. they're treating this like it's some kind of game when i need this job. like, sorry, man, but some of us aren't just getting jobs for "experience". i will not be able to afford tuition if you don't stop being an asshole about me typing "Bachelor of Fine Arts" instead of "Bachelor of Arts" (actual thing they rejected me for). "oh, well, they're just trying to prepare you for making resumés in the real world!" THIS IS THE REAL WORLD. I'M A REAL PERSON WHO NEEDS REAL FOOD AND REAL HOUSING. i literally cannot apply to jobs until they "approve" my resumé, meaning that all of the good/wellpaying jobs are getting taken up while i have to wait on some thumb-twiddling office goon to decide if they feel like ruining my life. if they want to "help" me, they can get out of my way and let me apply in bulk while there's still positions open.

ugh. i know that was a lot, i'm just really stressed out. i guess i just needed to get all that off my chest. i don't know what i'm gonna do if i can't get that work-study grant...

...i'll figure it out. somehow. i hope you know i'm thinking about you, every day.
