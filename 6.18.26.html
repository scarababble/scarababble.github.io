june 18, 2026 (thursday)

11:34 pm


as i'm writing this, i just got home from seeing you. we had big plans of going into town, but we went back to yours and dozed off together instead. no complaints from me.

i feel like it hasn't quite hit me yet that you're really leaving. i'm not looking forward to that sinking in. i plan on distracting myself- playing games, planning for college, and (of course) writing letters to you.

when the fall semester starts, we should have a sleepover to celebrate. i miss spending the night at your place. hey, with any luck, maybe you can spend the night at my place!
