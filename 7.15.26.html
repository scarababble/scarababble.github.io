july 15 (wednesday)

11:26 pm


it's officially been 10 days since i last got to talk to you. this really sucks.

i've been notating my music on the computer so that i have more tangible evidence of its existence (most of it is either scribbles in my notebook or stored in my noggin.) it's a very strange process. notating (for me) ends up being a lot less intuitive than composing a lot of the time because i compose at the piano. so i end up having to go back and pick apart what i was doing to enter it into the software. my hands know what they're doing, but my brain sometimes doesn't. it's like drawing something from memory- you think you know what it looks like, but then you sit down to try and sketch it out and realize you have no idea.

i'm really nervous about college stuff, but that's nothing new. i really wish i could talk to you right now, this is definitely a period of time where i need you even more than usual. love/miss you etc. stay safe out there.
