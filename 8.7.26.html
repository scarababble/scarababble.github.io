august 7 (friday)

7:41 pm


went to the thrift store with my sister today. i got a 100% cotton t-shirt, and a pair of wrangler jeans (also 100% cotton). between my purchases and hers, i spent more money than i meant to, but at least i got good-quality stuff.

when we left to head home, it started pouring rain- so much we could barely see in front of the windshield. we had no service, so i had to try and get us home with no map. we pulled into the library to maybe try and use their wifi. when we got there, though, they were closed, and we got soaked trying to go inside. i managed to get us home okay, and we had pancakes and hot cocoa to warm up after being damp for so long.
