july 8 (wednesday)

11:13 pm


today was a lazy day. i think i might be getting a little sick (knock on wood). i'm pounding back tea and chocolate and trying to nip it in the bud. i think i'll be fine.

for some reason, i can't get the mental image of you in pajamas out of my head. like when you're just in sweats, scrolling on your phone when you think i'm asleep. i think i just miss seeing you be comfortable. i definitely know i miss watching you do super mundane stuff, like tucking in your shirt or cooking breakfast or driving. there's something very reassuring about it.

i hope you're alright out there. not being able to text you sucks. i miss yapping with you. we both gotta hang in there- we can do it!
