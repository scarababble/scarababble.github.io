june 25 (thursday)

5:23 pm


i finally finished the new deltarune chapter and i'm trying so hard not to be upset. yeah, it had some genuinely good moments. but i really think i hate the entire rest of it.

the entire thing just felt like an ouroboros. start to finish, it was just making references to stuff that was in undertale/stuff that you'd only understand if you'd spent an unhealthy amount of time in deltarune internet spaces. it felt less like a chapter and more like it was just trying to give people fodder for fan content.

i'm going through other people's opinions, and the excuse i keep seeing everyone use is "well, he said it was going to be a light-hearted chapter!"
as if that somehow excuses lack of substance. everything about it felt shockingly insincere, like it was just trying to generate screenshots and start reddit arguments. i'm legit thinking i'm gonna take kind of a break from deltarune stuff for a while. it hurts my heart a little, because i was so exited.

maybe you'll play it and see something in it that i didn't. if not, at least i'll have someone to complain with.
...i really, really miss you. i miss talking to you. it's so lonely back here. nothing seems as fun anymore
these days...

anyway. i guess i should probably get started on cooking dinner for my family. i'm counting down the days until we can cook together again. take care of yourself for me, okay?
