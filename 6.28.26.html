june 28 (sunday)

7:46 pm


i can't believe it's only been 10 days since i started writing here. it feels like it's nearly been a month.
this sucks.

it's pretty quiet on the home front, not much to say. things are a little tense with my family just because mom's so stressed from work, but that's nothing out of the ordinary.

i wish we could go out. even if i couldn't stay the whole night with you, it'd be nice to get dinner and watch the sun set. you have a way of breathing fresh air into my life.
10 days down, 42 to go.
