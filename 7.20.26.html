july 20 (monday)

11:36 pm


i finally got to see you for the first time in a whole month!!!!!

the drive there and back was a little grueling, but went about as well as it possibly could. it was totally worth it getting to actually hug you.

i want to make you a home cooked dinner so bad it's not even funny. you need to get some meat back on your bones :[ i'm worried.
at least i got to give you those cookies. i'm glad you liked them. you also gave me (gasp) a letter! i haven't gotten to read it yet, but i'm excited to. i was itching so bad to tell you about this webpage, but i want it to be a surprise...!

here's to the halfway mark, i guess. i hope you know that i would happily drive 4 hours again right now if it meant i could see you again. the hotel they'll have you staying in for your internship looks pretty plush, so that's a relief. i hope when i pick you up from the airport in a month, you'll be fat and happy with a bunch of souvenirs in your duffel bag :]
