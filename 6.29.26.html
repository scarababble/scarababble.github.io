june 29 (monday)

8:08 pm


i'm so frazzled... got a lot done today, though.
i spoke to my advisor, and am now officially registered for all my classes. how am i going to pay for them? i don't know! apparently, i was wrong about my previous assessment of my financial aid- i was looking at the amount i got for fall and spring, not just fall.
in other words, i have like half the money i thought i did. i'm trying to figure out if i can even afford tuition, much less housing and stuff.

i went to the bank and deposited a bunch of cash that i had squirreled away in my room. it's not enough to magically fix all my problems, of course, but a couple hundred dollars is nothing to scoff at. i even raided my minecraft creeper plush piggybank for some quarters.

kind of at my wit's end a little bit, to be honest. it's not exactly like i can just magically pull thousands of dollars out of my ass, even if i deplete my savings. so i'm just trying to focus on what i have- which for right now, is a solid class schedule and... i guess, a can-do attitude. i'll call the financial aid offices first thing
in the morning.
