july 11 (saturday)

7:12 pm


spent an unreasonable amount of time today editing sheet music for a stranger. someone posted in the OFF sub that they were a beginner pianist and were uncertain how to play the sheet music they'd found of one of the songs. i check it out and it's one of those stupid ones where they basically treat the piano like a MIDI reader- fine for a computer to read, but literally impossible for a human being to play. there were parts that needed the right hand to stretch two octaves. no one can do that.

so i made a drastically simplified version. they haven't replied to it, but i hope they like it (it took like 2 hours). maybe i'll play it myself and upload it on here if i have the time.
