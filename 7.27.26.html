july 27 (monday)

10:43 pm


i'm slowly pulling myself out of the slump, i think. it's gonna take a while but i'm feeling better than i was.

i made a big pot roast for my family. the meat turned out a little tough for some reason but the texture on the potatoes was perfect. i also made a jam cake. it's really sweet but overall really pleasant. i wish you could have some.

Buri's in my lap as i write this, running his little motor. we used to say he had a huge purr for such a tiny kitty... but then he got huge, too.
i hope you get to see your cats when you get home. it's a pretty great feeling coming home to them after a long trip.

i'm thinking about you... i can't believe it's less than two weeks before i get to see you again. i'm gonna make you a ton of food and rub some knots out of your back. you've more than earned it.
